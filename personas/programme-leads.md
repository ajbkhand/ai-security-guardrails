# Programme leads checklist

This checklist applies to:
- Senior Responsible Owners (SROs)
- Programme leads
- Delivery group leads

Focus: **Accountability, risk, and governance**

---

# ✅ Concept stage

## You must:

- [ ] Define the business need and expected outcomes  
- [ ] Identify potential harms and public risks  
- [ ] Confirm whether AI is appropriate  
- [ ] Ensure DPIA is triggered where required  
- [ ] Ensure the project is registered in the AI Inventory  

## You are accountable for:

- Risk ownership  
- Ethical and lawful use  
- Public trust considerations  

---

# ✅ Incubate stage

## You must:

- [ ] Ensure data quality and provenance are assessed  
- [ ] Confirm security-by-design principles are applied  
- [ ] Ensure appropriate stakeholders are engaged:
  - Legal  
  - DPO  
  - Security  
  - Architecture  

- [ ] Confirm delivery route (DASH, CDP, CCoE, supplier)  
- [ ] Ensure funding covers security, testing, and assurance  

## You are accountable for:

- Governance being embedded early  
- Delivery approach alignment with Defra standards  

---

# ✅ Pilot stage

## You must:

- [ ] Ensure DPIA is completed  
- [ ] Ensure ATRS is published (if required)  
- [ ] Ensure red-teaming and robustness testing are completed  
- [ ] Confirm human oversight is in place  
- [ ] Ensure disclosures and transparency are implemented  

## STOP if:

- Testing is incomplete  
- Governance artefacts are missing  
- Risks are not mitigated  

---

# ✅ Scale stage

## You must:

- [ ] Ensure monitoring and incident response are operational  
- [ ] Ensure ongoing evaluation (accuracy, bias, drift)  
- [ ] Maintain ATRS and disclosures  
- [ ] Ensure change control is in place  

## You are accountable for:

- Ongoing safe operation  
- Continuous assurance  

---

# ✅ Summary

You are accountable for ensuring that AI systems are:

- Lawful  
- Safe  
- Transparent  
- Governed proportionately to risk
