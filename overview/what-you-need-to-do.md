# What you need to do

This page summarises the **minimum actions required** to deliver AI safely in Defra.

Use this as a quick reference before starting or approving any AI work.

---

# 1. Confirm AI is appropriate

Before starting, you must:

- Define the user need and expected outcome  
- Confirm AI is necessary (not a simpler solution)  
- Identify risks and potential harms  

✅ If AI is not required → do not proceed  

---

# 2. Identify risk level

Ask:

- Will this be used externally?  
- Does it influence decisions about people or public outcomes?  
- Does it use sensitive data?  

---

## Apply the correct guardrail tier

| Situation | Required controls |
|----------|------------------|
| All AI systems | Tier 2 (minimum controls) |
| Public / external systems | Tier 1 (transparency + testing) |
| High-risk or decision impact | Tier 0 (enhanced assurance) |

---

# 3. Select delivery approach

Choose your platform:

- DASH  
- CDP  
- CCoE  
- Supplier  

✅ Platform does not change what controls apply — only how you implement them  

---

# 4. Follow the lifecycle

You must apply controls at each stage:

- Concept → define risks and need  
- Incubate → design securely  
- Pilot → test and validate  
- Scale → monitor and govern  

➡️ See: Lifecycle  

---

# 5. Apply security controls

All AI systems must:

- Use trusted and validated data  
- Apply least privilege access  
- Encrypt data at rest and in transit  
- Enable logging and monitoring  
- Implement incident response  

✅ Controls must be proportionate to risk  

---

# 6. Test before release

For Pilot and beyond, you must:

- Conduct red-teaming (if public or high-risk)  
- Test for:
  - Prompt injection  
  - Data leakage  
  - Bias and fairness  
- Validate robustness under misuse  

✅ Do not go live without testing  

---

# 7. Ensure transparency and oversight

Where required, you must:

- Provide user disclosures  
- Maintain human oversight or fallback  
- Publish an ATRS (if applicable)  

✅ Users must understand how AI is used  

---

# 8. Monitor and maintain

At Scale, you must:

- Monitor performance and drift  
- Detect misuse or anomalies  
- Maintain logs and audit trails  
- Update controls and documentation  

✅ AI systems must remain safe over time  

---

# 9. Fulfil your role responsibilities

Different roles must:

- Own risk (programme leads)  
- Embed controls (project managers)  
- Implement security (technologists)  

➡️ See: Personas  

---

# STOP conditions

Do NOT proceed if:

- The use case is unclear  
- AI is not necessary  
- Risks are not understood  
- Required controls cannot be implemented  

---

# What good looks like

An AI system in Defra is:

- Secure by design  
- Tested before release  
- Transparent to users  
- Proportionate to risk  
- Continuously monitored  

---

# Summary


All AI projects must:

1. Confirm AI is appropriate  
2. Apply the correct guardrail tier  
3. Follow the lifecycle  
4. Implement security controls  
5. Test thoroughly  
6. Monitor continuously  

> AI must be safe, controlled, and accountable from design through to operation.
