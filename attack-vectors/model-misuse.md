# Model misuse or repurposing

## What it is

A model is used outside its intended purpose, creating risk.

## Risks

- Incorrect decisions  
- Ethical or compliance issues  
- Loss of public trust  

## Defra example

A risk model is reused for policy decisions without proper context or validation.

## When it occurs

Primarily at:

- Scale stage  

---

## Required controls

You must:

- Restrict model access (RBAC)  
- Define intended use clearly (model cards)  
- Monitor usage patterns  
- Detect unusual or unauthorised use  

---

## What good looks like

- Models are used only for approved purposes  
- Usage is monitored and controlled  
- Limitations are clearly documented
