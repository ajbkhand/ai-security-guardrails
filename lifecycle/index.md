# AI lifecycle

AI systems in Defra follow a standard lifecycle:

- Concept  
- Incubate  
- Pilot  
- Scale  

Security controls must be applied at **each stage**.

---

## Lifecycle overview

### Concept

Define:
- User need  
- Risks and harms  
- Whether AI is appropriate  

---

### Incubate

Design:
- Secure architecture  
- Data quality and provenance  
- Identity and access controls  

---

### Pilot

Validate:
- Security controls  
- Bias and robustness  
- Safe deployment  

---

### Scale

Operate:
- Monitoring and drift detection  
- Incident response  
- Continuous assurance  

---

## Key principle

Controls are **progressive**:

| Stage | Focus |
|------|------|
| Concept | Define and assess risk |
| Incubate | Design securely |
| Pilot | Test and validate |
| Scale | Monitor and govern |

---

## Critical requirement

The **technical security standard becomes mandatory at Pilot and Scale**, and earlier for high-risk systems. [1](https://defra.sharepoint.com/teams/Team4303/SitePages/Defra-Group-AI-Security-Guardrails.aspx)
