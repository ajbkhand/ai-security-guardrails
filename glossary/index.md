# Glossary

## AI workload

A system that trains, deploys, or operates AI models, including data pipelines and infrastructure.

---

## LLM (Large Language Model)

A model trained on large text datasets to generate natural language outputs.

---

## SLM (Small Language Model)

A smaller, more controlled model with reduced cost and risk.

---

## Prompt injection

An attack that manipulates model inputs to override system instructions.

---

## ATRS

Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard.

A public record describing how AI systems are used.

---

## DPIA

Data Protection Impact Assessment.

A process to assess privacy risks.

---

## RBAC

Role-Based Access Control.

Restricts system access based on roles.

---

## Model drift

Changes in model performance over time due to changing data.

---

## Data poisoning

Insertion of malicious or incorrect data into training datasets.

---

## Red-teaming

Testing systems under adversarial conditions to identify weaknesses.
