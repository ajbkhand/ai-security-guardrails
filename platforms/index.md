# Platforms

The guardrails apply to all platforms used for AI workloads.

## Supported platforms

- DASH (Databricks)  
- CDP (Core Delivery Platform)  
- CCoE (Cloud Centre of Excellence)  
- Supplier-hosted infrastructure  
- Geospatial platforms  
- HPC / research environments  

---

## Key principle

> Platform determines how controls are implemented, not whether they apply.

---

## What you inherit vs what you add

### Platforms provide:

- Identity and access control  
- Networking and encryption  
- Logging and monitoring  
- Baseline governance  

---

### You must still implement:

- Data quality and provenance checks  
- Model evaluation and bias testing  
- AI-specific controls (prompt injection, misuse)  
- ATRS and transparency (where required)  

---

## Example

A model deployed on DASH inherits:

- Identity and logging  

But must add:

- Bias testing  
- Model documentation  
- Red-teaming (if public-facing)  

---

## Outcome

All platforms must result in:

- Secure and controlled AI workloads  
- Consistent governance across Defra
