# AI red-teaming

Red-teaming tests AI systems under **adversarial conditions** to identify weaknesses.

It is a core part of **trusted design**.

---

## When required

You must perform red-teaming when:

- Systems are public-facing  
- Systems influence decisions  
- Systems operate at scale  
- Risk is high  

---

## When to run

- Before release (Pilot stage)  
- Periodically during Scale  

---

## What to test

- Prompt injection  
- Data exfiltration  
- Abuse and misuse  
- Bias and fairness under pressure  

---

## Who should be involved

- Technical experts  
- Domain specialists  
- Policy and legal teams  
- External or non-technical users  

---

## Outputs

A red-team exercise must produce:

- Exploit scenarios  
- Identified risks  
- Mitigations applied  
- Updates to ATRS  

---

## Example

For a chatbot:

- Attempt to override instructions  
- Attempt to extract sensitive data  
- Test harmful or unsafe outputs  

---

## Key principle

> Red-teaming is not optional for high-risk AI systems.
