# Follow the lifecycle

You must complete these steps at each stage of AI delivery.

---

# Concept: define the problem

You must:

- ✅ Define the user need and outcome  
- ✅ Identify risks and potential harms  
- ✅ Confirm AI is necessary  
- ✅ Check if a DPIA is required  
- ✅ Register the project  

### Do not proceed if:

- ❌ The use case is unclear  
- ❌ Risks are not understood  
- ❌ AI is not necessary  

---

# Incubate: design the solution

You must:

- ✅ Validate data quality and provenance  
- ✅ Apply least privilege access  
- ✅ Encrypt data (at rest and in transit)  
- ✅ Enable logging and audit  
- ✅ Define model purpose and limitations  

### Do

- ✅ Design securely from the start  
- ✅ Validate datasets  

---

### Do not

- ❌ Use untrusted data  
- ❌ Skip security design  

---

# Pilot: test before release

You must:

- ✅ Complete a DPIA  
- ✅ Implement human oversight  
- ✅ Test for:
  - Prompt injection  
  - Bias and fairness  
  - Misuse scenarios  

- ✅ Perform red-teaming (if required)  
- ✅ Provide user disclosures  

### Do not go live if:

- ❌ Testing is incomplete  
- ❌ Risks are not mitigated  
- ❌ Controls are missing  

---

# Scale: operate safely

You must:

- ✅ Monitor performance and drift  
- ✅ Detect misuse or anomalies  
- ✅ Maintain logs and audit trails  
- ✅ Update disclosures and ATRS  
- ✅ Apply change control  

### Do

- ✅ Continuously monitor systems  
- ✅ Revalidate performance and safety  

---

### Do not

- ❌ Assume systems remain safe over time  
- ❌ Ignore drift or misuse  

---

# Summary

You must:

- Define risks early  
- Design securely  
- Test before release  
- Monitor continuously  

> If it is not tested and monitored, it is not safe to use.
