# Checklists

Checklists translate guardrails into **practical actions**.

They ensure:

- Consistency  
- Auditability  
- Compliance  

---

## Available checklists

- Lifecycle checklists  
- Role-based (persona) checklists  

---

## How to use

1. Identify your lifecycle stage  
2. Identify your role  
3. Complete all checklist items  
4. Provide evidence where required  

---

## Key principle

> If it is not evidenced, it is not done.

---

## What good looks like

- All checklist items completed  
- Evidence recorded  
- Risks understood and mitigated  
- Controls applied proportionately
